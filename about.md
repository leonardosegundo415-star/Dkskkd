# Frame Counter Free

A free, open and **mobile-friendly** (Android 64-bit / 32-bit and Windows) frame counter for Geometry Dash.

- Live **frame** and level-time counter, plus your real display **FPS**
- **Per-click timing**: the gap before every press and the hold of every release, in frames and milliseconds
- **Counter rows** (default `1,2,3,4,5+`) with colours, fully configurable
- Count at **60 / 120 / 240 FPS** (or any value) without touching the game's speed
- Optional **click sounds** (tighter click = lower pitch) and **input display**
- **CSV export** of the whole session
- **Macros**: every completed run is recorded; **Replay** it from the pause menu
- **Scan windows**: moves each click earlier/later to measure how many frames of margin it has (numbers shown on replay + CSV)
- Pause-menu button to show/hide the HUD with one tap

It never connects to the internet, needs no licence and does not modify gameplay.

## More
- Filters: straight fly, wave spam, useless clicks; NaN GD preset
- Scan part (% of level), time limit, resume, incomplete runs
- Hitboxes, hitboxes on death, record / replay speed
- Custom sounds (1-9 frames + wide) and counter colours
- High FPS: pair it with a TPS bypass; step length is measured automatically
